Race-week events & fan experiences

The circuit — Lusail at night

5.419 km of fast, flowing corners — 16 of them — run entirely under floodlights. One of the quickest laps on the calendar, and a genuine overtaking chance into Turn 1 at the end of the long main straight.

Two stops, minimum

Tyres are capped at 27 laps per set this weekend, so nobody can one-stop: every driver must pit at least twice in the 57-lap race. Strategy stays live to the flag.

Formula 2 — the support bill

Formula 2 shares the weekend, with its own feature and sprint races under the same lights — the next generation, on the same stage, all three days.

Title watch — the fight for second

The drivers' title is already Antonelli's. What remains is second place: Verstappen leads Hamilton by 37 points and can settle it here, a round early.

Tickets at a glance (official face value, 3-day)

Official tickets

General admission from QAR 600 · Main grandstand QAR 2,000 — official prices, straight from the circuit's own ticket shop.

Where to sit

The main grandstand faces the pit straight and the Turn 1 braking zone — the race's main overtaking spot. General admission banks give the flowing middle sector at the lowest price.

Practical notes

Getting there

The circuit sits just north of Doha. The Doha Metro plus event shuttle buses is the standard way in on race days — driving yourself means desert-road traffic after the sessions.

Entry rules

Bag checks at the gates and the usual circuit rules on banners and professional cameras — check the official FAQ before you go, especially for camera gear.

Weather

Late-November Doha is mild by day and cooler after sunset. The race runs at night, so bring a light layer for the grandstand.

Why Qatar

A purpose-built night circuit with a MotoGP heart: fast, flowing, and lit like a stadium. As the penultimate round, it is where the season's last fights get settled.
