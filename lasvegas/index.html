Race-week events & fan experiences

F1 Academy — all three nights

The all-female single-seater series races in Las Vegas too — practice and qualifying on Thursday, a reverse-grid race on Friday, and the feature race on Saturday afternoon before the Grand Prix.

When · Thu–Sat, between F1 sessions

Trackside stages — with your ticket

Two stages run inside the circuit, and your zone ticket decides which one you get. T-Mobile Stage (T-Mobile Zone at Sphere): Two Friends & Ja Rule on Thursday, Disclosure & Gorgon City on Friday, Sean Paul & Natasha Bedingfield on Saturday. Heineken Stage (East Harmon): The Australian Bee Gees on Thursday, Icona Pop & Eli & Fur on Friday, Disco Dom & LP Giobbi on Saturday — plus live Q&A sessions with the F1 drivers across the weekend.

The Strip circuit

A 6.201 km street circuit down Las Vegas Boulevard — past Caesars Palace, the Bellagio fountains and the Venetian, with the start line at Harmon Avenue & Koval Lane. Around 2 km of flat-out running down the Strip.

Heineken Silver Main (East Harmon) sits opposite the pits and the start/finish straight. Heineken Grandstands at Turn 4 are 21+ with all-inclusive beer. Every grandstand is open-air — and Flamingo Zone 3-day passes are already sold out on the official site.

Practical notes

Getting there

Fly into LAS (Harry Reid International) — about 10 minutes from the Strip by car. The circuit is the Strip itself, so most hotels are a walk from a zone. Roads around the circuit close every afternoon on session days (in 2025, from 1 PM); pedestrian bridges stay open for crossing, not for watching.

Entry rules

Tickets are zone-specific — your ticket locks you to one zone, so pick your view before you buy. Three-day passes are the standard; single-day Thursday tickets are the cheapest way to sample the weekend.

Weather

Race-week forecast (checked Nov 14): highs around 15°C (59°F), night lows 5–7°C (41–45°F), with showers possible on Thursday. Sunset is 4:29 PM and every session runs after dark — bring a real jacket for the open-air grandstands, and a light rain layer for Thursday night.

Why Vegas

The only Saturday-night race on the US calendar, run down the most famous street in America. F1 is in Las Vegas through 2037 after the ten-year extension signed in June 2026.
