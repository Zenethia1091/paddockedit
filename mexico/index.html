Race-week events & fan experiences

The Foro Sol stadium section

The famous stadium section — Grandstands 14 (South) and 15 (North) sit inside the old Foro Sol baseball stadium, and the podium ceremony happens right there in front of the crowd. The loudest corner of the weekend.

Grandstands · 14 / 15Face value · MXN 10,000

Title watch — Antonelli can clinch it here

Kimi Antonelli arrives with the championship almost in hand — a ninth-place finish, just two points, seals the title whatever Russell and Hamilton do. If it happens, he becomes the youngest world champion in F1 history at 20.

Needs · P9 — two points

Sergio Pérez — 300th Grand Prix at home

Home hero Sergio Pérez marks his 300th Grand Prix start in front of his home crowd, back in F1 with Cadillac. He opens the week with a free show run on Paseo de la Reforma on Wednesday, Oct 28.

Show run · Oct 28 · Paseo de la Reforma

The altitude factor

At more than 2,200m, this is the highest circuit on the calendar. The thin air punishes cooling and downforce — and regularly shakes up the competitive order, which is why Mexico so often produces a surprise.

Altitude · 2,200m+Highest · On the calendar

Pit Lane WalksGOOD TO KNOW

There is no public pit lane walk at the Hermanos Rodríguez. Pit lane walks run Friday–Sunday for Paddock Club guests only, plus a Thursday walk for selected F1 Experiences guests.

Access · Hospitality guests only

Race weekend recap

Verstappen won from P5 — his sixth Mexico win; Hamilton P2; Antonelli P3, sealing the 2026 world championship at 20 years old, the youngest champion in F1 history.

Where to sit

Foro Sol (Grandstands 14/15) is the atmosphere pick — the podium happens right in front of you. Main Grandstand 1/2 faces the pits and the start/finish straight. Tickets are zone-specific — your ticket locks you to one colour-coded zone (Green, Orange, Blue, Yellow, Grey, Brown), so pick your view before you buy. The official site showed a buy button for 10 grandstands in September.

Practical notes

Getting there

Fly into MEX (Mexico City International). The circuit sits inside the Magdalena Mixhuca sports city — take Metro Line 9 to Ciudad Deportiva, Velódromo or Puebla stations. There is no public parking at the circuit.

Entry rules

Gates open around 08:00, and there is no re-entry. Tickets are sold as 3-day passes — one ticket covers the whole weekend.

Weather

Late October in Mexico City: days in the mid-20s°C, nights around 11–12°C. Afternoon showers have been in the forecast for Friday and Saturday, with Sunday looking dry — check again in race week.

Why Mexico

More than 400,000 fans across the weekend in 2025, and the loudest podium on the calendar. F1's highest race, inside a stadium, in one of the world's great food cities.
