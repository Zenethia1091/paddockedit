Race-week events & fan experiences

The circuit — Yas Marina at twilight

5.281 km wrapped around a marina and a hotel the cars pass under. The race starts in daylight and ends under floodlights — no other circuit changes mood mid-race like this one.

The season finale

The last race of 2026: final points, final goodbyes, and the last word on the championship order — everything the season built toward, settled in one evening.

Formula 2 — title decider

Formula 2 also ends its season at Yas Marina, with the championship decided on the same weekend — two finales for the price of one.

My 2025 race

I closed my 2025 season at this race, trackside at Yas Marina — it is in my Grand Prix Log. The finale atmosphere here is part party, part farewell.

Tickets at a glance (official face value, 3-day)

Official tickets

General admission from AED 745 on the official site, with grandstand and hospitality options alongside. The finale sells out early most years.

Where to sit

The main grandstand watches the start and the pit stops; the marina-side stands catch the day-to-night change at its best. For the finish-line moment, sit as close to the start straight as you can.

Practical notes

Getting there

Yas Island sits minutes from Abu Dhabi's airport. Taxis and event buses are the standard way in; the island was built for event crowds, so race-day flow is smooth.

Entry rules

Standard circuit gate checks apply. The official site carries the full list of permitted items — worth a look before packing camera gear.

Weather

Early-December Abu Dhabi is warm by day and pleasantly cool by night. The twilight start means one outfit has to cover both — layers work.

Why Abu Dhabi

The calendar's permanent finale: a title-decider venue with a party island around it. When the season has one last story to tell, it tells it here.
