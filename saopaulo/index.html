Race-week events & fan experiences

F1 Fan Zone — Drivers' Engagement

Kartódromo Ayrton Senna, inside the circuit grounds. The Fan Zone stage is where drivers appear for interviews and fan engagement across the weekend — in 2025 the sessions ran Friday ~09:20 and Saturday ~08:35; 2026 times are announced during race week. Concerts and a giant LED screen (no track view) fill the rest of the day.

Entry · Free for Sector G/H/K/N/R & Porto Bank ticket holders (capacity limited)When · All weekend

Heineken Village

Heineken's official hospitality lawns inside the circuit — Gramado (open lawn) and Estrela (premium) — with food, drinks and big screens between sessions. Sold as 3-day passes on Eventim.

Face value · Gramado R$2,480 / Estrela R$4,960Tickets · Eventim

Porto Bank Grandstand

Porto's own grandstand at Interlagos. Porto also backs Brazil's home driver Gabriel Bortoleto — expect the loudest corner of the circuit when the Sauber/Audi car passes.

Face value · R$2,500Tickets · Eventim

Pit Lane WalksGOOD TO KNOW

There is no public pit lane walk at Interlagos. Pit lane walks run on Friday–Sunday at allotted times for Paddock Club and hospitality guests only, plus a Thursday walk for selected F1 Experiences guests.

Access · Hospitality guests only

Official Box Office — last face-value window

Av. Senador Teotônio Vilela, next to Gate 7, Interlagos. Open from October 30: Oct 30–Nov 5, 09:00–17:00; Nov 6–7, 07:00–17:00; Nov 8, 07:00–14:00. Whatever remains goes on sale in person at face value.

Race weekend recap

Norris took pole in a rain-delayed qualifying and won the race; Verstappen charged from P21 to P2; Mercedes sealed the constructors' championship. Two Brazilians — Bortoleto and Câmara — raced at home.

Tickets at a glance (official face value, 3-day)

Sector G R$990 · Sector A R$1,590 · Sector R R$2,150 · Sector M R$3,550 · Sector D R$4,680 · Sector B R$5,290 · F1 Fanzone R$620. Official sale is 3-day passes only, via Eventim; most grandstands sold out within minutes — resale and the Oct 30 box office are the remaining routes.

Only Sectors B and M have assigned seats — everywhere else is unreserved, so arrive early for the spot you want. The podium sits on top of the pit building, facing Sectors B and M.

Practical notes

Getting there

Fly into GRU (Guarulhos) for all international flights — the circuit is roughly 45 km away. On race days the standard route is CPTM Line 9 (Emerald) to Autódromo station, then the signposted walk to the gates. Stations get extremely busy after the race; build in buffer time.

Entry rules

Gates 08:00, entry until mid-afternoon. One entry per day — no re-entry except VIP/hospitality. Bags larger than 25×25×25 cm are not allowed; no outside liquids; up to 3 sealed food items; sunscreen up to 100 ml; no umbrellas or selfie sticks; payments inside are cashless.

Weather

Early November is the start of São Paulo's rainy season and afternoon storms are an Interlagos classic — several famous races were turned upside down by them. Pack a poncho (umbrellas are banned inside) and expect plans to flex.

Why Interlagos

One of the few anti-clockwise circuits on the calendar, and Ayrton Senna's home track. Cars pass every ~70 seconds on the short lap, the Senna S is the prime overtaking spot, and the crowd sings from Friday morning.
